# Packet checks

150 stripped personality profiles; 146 adult assignments (130 workers and 16 exploratory staff); four excluded minors; 54 complete questions. All 21 English DASS statements match the supplied original PDF after whitespace normalization. Nepali original PDF/image supplied without retyping damaged text extraction. Research hypotheses, literature and target associations were not copied into the packet.

Structural test fixtures were generated only in temporary directories and removed. They are NOT participant answers or agent-trial results. Tests checked item coverage, ranges, invalid DASS codes, multiple-choice exclusivity, factual demographics, null handling, incomplete-subscale scoring, staff B02 routing, simulated refusal, complete/incomplete run handling, and rejection of changed frozen records.

No respondent subagents have been run. The packet contains no generated questionnaire-response dataset. Structural checks cannot establish realism, translation validity or whether any real-world hypothesis is correct.
